Naciones y pueblos indígena originario campesinos in Bolivia: where to start

If you are Quechua, Aymara or from another of Bolivia's Indigenous peoples, this page is just beginning: it has background on land, rights and recent events from The Indigenous World.

Bolivia recognizes 36 Indigenous peoples and calls itself a plurinational state. In the 2012 census 41% of people over 15 were of Indigenous origin, and the national statistics office projected 48% by 2017. Most Indigenous people in the Andes are Quechua or Aymara.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Bolivia.

Background: read the Bolivia chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.
