Adivasi peoples in Bangladesh: where to start

If you are Adivasi, in the plains or in the Chittagong Hill Tracts, this page is just beginning: it has background on land, rights and recent events from The Indigenous World.

More than 54 Indigenous peoples live in Bangladesh, speaking at least 35 languages. The 2022 census counted about 1.65 million Indigenous people (1%), while Indigenous peoples put their own number near 4 million. Most live in the plains; the rest live in the Chittagong Hill Tracts.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Bangladesh.

Background: read the Bangladesh chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.
