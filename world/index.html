First Peoples around the world

Nina's Map began in the United States. These pages carry it to other countries, one at a time, using each people's own terms. Every listing shows where it came from and when it was checked.

Country pages so far: Canada, México, Australia, Aotearoa New Zealand, Sápmi (Norway, Sweden, Finland), Brasil, Perú, Guatemala, Chile, Kalaallit Nunaat (Greenland), Taiwan, Japan, Philippines, Botswana, Namibia and South Africa, and help that reaches Indigenous peoples anywhere. Some pages are starters, with background and the help we could confirm; more are on the way. If you want your country next, or know what belongs on its page, tell us.
