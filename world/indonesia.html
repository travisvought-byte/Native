Indonesia: help and community for Masyarakat Adat

If you belong to a Masyarakat Adat community, start with AMAN, the national alliance of Indigenous peoples, and its youth (BPAN) and women's (Perempuan AMAN) bodies.

AMAN, the national alliance of Indigenous peoples (Aliansi Masyarakat Adat Nusantara), represents 2,596 communities with about 21 million members. AMAN estimates that 50 to 70 million Indigenous people live in Indonesia.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Indonesia.

Background: read the Indonesia chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Peoples, Nations and their governments

AMAN, Aliansi Masyarakat Adat Nusantara

For Indigenous people

The national alliance of Indigenous peoples in Indonesia. It works for the rights of Masyarakat Adat through its programmes, policy work and campaigns, and has affiliated bodies for youth (BPAN) and women (Perempuan AMAN).

Who it is for
Indigenous communities (Masyarakat Adat) across Indonesia.
How to start
Use the Kontak Kami (Contact Us) page on the AMAN website to reach the alliance.
