Sápmi (Norway, Sweden, Finland): help and community for Sámi

The Sámi are an Indigenous people whose homeland, Sápmi, stretches across northern Norway, Sweden, Finland and Russia. Each of the three Nordic countries has an elected Sámi Parliament. The Sámi Parliament of Finland notes that the Sámi are the only Indigenous people in the European Union.

Sources: Sámi Parliament of Norway · Sámi Parliament of Finland.

Recovery and wellness

SANKS: Sámi mental health and substance use service

For Indigenous people Kárášjohka/Karasjok, Norway

A specialist service of Finnmark Hospital Trust that gives Sámi people care in mental health and substance use that fits their language and culture, for children, youth and adults. It also helps people who have been subjected to violence or abuse.

Who it is for
Sámi people anywhere in Norway.
How to start
Ask your doctor, psychologist or child welfare service for a referral. Read about SANKS on Dinutvei, the national site on violence and abuse.

Sámediggi / Sametinget: the Sámi Parliament of Norway

Open to anyone Kárášjohka/Karasjok, Norway

The Sámi people's elected parliament in Norway, dealing with all matters that concern the Sámi. Its plenary meetings are open to the public, and it offers guided tours.

Who it is for
Sámi in Norway, and anyone with a question about Sámi matters.
How to start
Call or email the Sámi Parliament.
Address
Ávjovárgeaidnu 50, Kárášjohka/Karasjok, Norway

Call +47 78 47 40 00 Email samediggi@samediggi.no sametinget.no

Checked November 20, 2026. Source

Sámediggi / Sametinget: the Sámi Parliament of Sweden

Open to anyone Giron/Kiruna, Sweden

The elected Sámi Parliament in Sweden, based in Kiruna. Under Swedish law its main task is to look after matters of Sámi culture in Sweden.

Who it is for
Sámi in Sweden, and anyone with a question about Sámi matters.
How to start
Contact the Sámi Parliament through its website.
Address
Box 90, 981 22 Kiruna, Sweden

sametinget.se

Checked November 20, 2026. Source

Sámediggi / Saamelaiskäräjät: the Sámi Parliament of Finland

Open to anyone Anár/Inari, Finland

The elected Sámi Parliament in Finland, at the Sámi Cultural Centre Sajos in Inari. It works for Sámi rights, including in social and health services, and on Sámi languages, culture and youth.

Who it is for
Sámi in Finland, and anyone with a question about Sámi matters.
How to start
Call the telephone centre, Monday to Friday 9 to 11 and 12 to 15, or email.
Address
Menesjärventie 2 A, 99870 Inari, Finland

Call +358 10 839 3100 Email info@samediggi.fi samediggi.fi

Checked November 20, 2026. Source
