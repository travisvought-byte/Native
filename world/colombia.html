Colombia: help and community for Pueblos indígenas

Colombia is home to some 115 Indigenous peoples, living on the Caribbean and Pacific coasts, in the Amazon, on the Orinoco plains and in the Andes. In 2025 a decree created Indigenous Territorial Entities, Indigenous governments with powers over health, education, justice and land.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Colombia · The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Inter-American Commission on Human Rights.

Background: read the Colombia chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Crisis and safety

Línea 106: teleorientación en salud mental

Open to anyone

Línea nacional del Ministerio de Salud, gratuita y confidencial, las 24 horas, para apoyo emocional, crisis o simplemente hablar con un profesional de salud mental. (Free, confidential national mental health line, 24 hours.)

Who it is for
Cualquier persona en Colombia. (Anyone in Colombia.)
How to start
Llame al 106.

Call 106 minsalud.gov.co: Línea 106

Peoples, Nations and their governments

ONIC: Organización Nacional Indígena de Colombia

For Indigenous people Bogotá

La organización nacional de los pueblos indígenas de Colombia, nacida del primer congreso indígena nacional de 1982. Se presenta como autoridad de gobierno, justicia, derecho y representación de los pueblos indígenas. (The national organization of Colombia's Indigenous peoples, founded in 1982.)

Who it is for
Pueblos y organizaciones indígenas de Colombia.
How to start
Escriba a la ONIC o visite su sitio web.
Address
Calle 12B No. 4-38, Bogotá

Email onic@onic.org.co onic.org.co

Checked November 20, 2026. Source
