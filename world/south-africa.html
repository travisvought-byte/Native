South Africa: help and community for Khoikhoi and San (Khoisan)

If you are Khoikhoi or San, this page is a start: background on land, rights and recent events from The Indigenous World, and a free suicide crisis line.

The Khoikhoi and San peoples (the Khoisan) identify as South Africa's Indigenous peoples. Their largest representative bodies are the National Khoisan Council and the San Council of South Africa. Full legal recognition is still incomplete.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): South Africa.

Background: read the South Africa chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Crisis and safety

SADAG Suicide Crisis Helpline

Open to anyone

South Africa's suicide crisis line, run by the South African Depression and Anxiety Group: free, 24 hours a day, with referrals to local help.

Who it is for
Anyone in South Africa.
How to start
Call 0800 567 567, any time.
