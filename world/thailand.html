Thailand: help and community for Indigenous peoples

Indigenous peoples in Thailand live across the northern highlands, the western forests and the southern coasts; estimates of their number range from 1 to 6 million. Thailand endorsed the UN Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples but does not legally recognize Indigenous peoples as a distinct group, and many face statelessness and insecure land rights.

Sources: Minority Rights Group: Indigenous peoples in Thailand.

Crisis and safety

Mental Health Hotline 1323

Open to anyone

The Department of Mental Health (Ministry of Public Health) runs this free, confidential hotline 24 hours a day, every day, for stress, anxiety, sadness, loss and crisis. Counselling psychologists answer.

Who it is for
Anyone in Thailand. Help is given in Thai.
How to start
Call 1323 at any time. You can also book a call-back on the 1323 website.
