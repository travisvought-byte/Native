Perú: help and community for Pueblos indígenas

Peru officially recognizes 47 Indigenous languages, spoken by 55 peoples. In the 2017 census almost six million people, just over a quarter of the population, identified as Indigenous; most identified as Quechua or Aymara.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Peru.

Background: read the Perú chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Crisis and safety

Línea 113 Salud, opción 5 (salud mental)

Open to anyone

Orientación y apoyo psicológico gratuito del Ministerio de Salud, las 24 horas, todos los días. (Free psychological support from the Ministry of Health, 24 hours.)

Who it is for
Cualquier persona en el Perú. (Anyone in Peru.)
How to start
Marque 113 desde cualquier teléfono y elija la opción 5.
