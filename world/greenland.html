Kalaallit Nunaat (Greenland): help and community for Kalaallit (Greenlandic Inuit)

Kalaallit Nunaat has governed itself since 1979 within the Kingdom of Denmark. Of its 56,740 people (February 2026), 88.9% are Greenlandic Inuit, most of whom call themselves Kalaallit. About 17,000 Kalaallit live in Denmark.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Kalaallit Nunaat (Greenland).

Background: read the Kalaallit Nunaat (Greenland) chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Naalakkersuisut: Government of Greenland

Open to anyone Nuuk

Greenland's self-government, with ministries for fisheries and hunting, housing, education, culture and more.

Who it is for
People in Greenland, and Kalaallit anywhere with a question for the government.
How to start
Call the central switchboard or visit the website.

Call +299 34 50 00 naalakkersuisut.gl
