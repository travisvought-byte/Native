Worldwide: help and community for Indigenous peoples everywhere

If you are Indigenous anywhere, or your community is organizing, these reach across borders: grants for projects your community designs itself, the UN Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues, and a yearly report on where things stand in each country.

Help that reaches Indigenous peoples in any country: international bodies and funds led by Indigenous people.

Food, money and bills

Keepers of the Earth Fund (Cultural Survival)

For Indigenous people

Grants of up to 12,000 US dollars that go straight to Indigenous communities, collectives, organizations and traditional governments for projects they design themselves. Groups without government registration can apply.

Who it is for
Indigenous communities and groups in any country.
How to start
Read the fund's page and watch for its yearly call for proposals.

UN Permanent Forum on Indigenous Issues

Open to anyone

The United Nations advisory body on Indigenous peoples' issues: development, culture, environment, education, health and human rights. It meets each year in New York, where Indigenous peoples' organizations can take part.

Who it is for
Indigenous peoples and their organizations everywhere.
How to start
Read about the Forum and how to take part in its yearly session.

The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA)

Open to anyone

A yearly report, free to read, on the situation and rights of Indigenous peoples in more than 60 countries, written mostly by Indigenous and expert authors. The 2026 edition is the 40th.

Who it is for
Anyone who wants to know where things stand for Indigenous peoples in a country.
How to start
Open the report and choose a country.
