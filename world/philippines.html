Philippines: help and community for Indigenous Cultural Communities / Indigenous Peoples

If you belong to one of the Indigenous Cultural Communities / Indigenous Peoples, from the Lumad of Mindanao to the peoples of Luzon and the Visayas, start with the National Commission on Indigenous Peoples, which handles rights and ancestral domain titles under the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act.

The 2020 census counted 9,841,785 Indigenous people in the Philippines: 63% in Mindanao, where the Lumad are the largest group, 34% in Luzon and 3% in the Visayas. The National Commission on Indigenous Peoples was created under the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Philippines.

Background: read the Philippines chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Crisis and safety

NCMH Crisis Hotline

Open to anyone

The 24-hour mental health crisis line of the National Center for Mental Health, part of the Department of Health, for anyone in emotional distress or thinking about suicide.

Who it is for
Anyone in the Philippines.
How to start
Dial 1553 from a landline (toll-free), or 1800-1888-1553.

Call 1553 doh.gov.ph

Peoples, Nations and their governments

National Commission on Indigenous Peoples (NCIP)

For Indigenous people Quezon City

The national government agency that protects the rights of Indigenous Cultural Communities and Indigenous Peoples, including ancestral domain titles, under the Indigenous Peoples' Rights Act of 1997.

Who it is for
Indigenous peoples in the Philippines.
How to start
Email the central office or contact the NCIP office for your region.
Address
6th and 7th Floors, Sunnymede IT Center, 1614 Quezon Avenue, South Triangle, Quezon City

Email osec@ncip.gov.ph ncip.gov.ph

Checked November 20, 2026. Source
