Cambodia: help and community for Indigenous peoples

Cambodia is home to 24 different Indigenous peoples, who make up 2 to 3% of the national population.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Cambodia.

Background: read the Cambodia chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.
