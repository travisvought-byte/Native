Brasil: help and community for Povos indígenas

If you are Indigenous in Brazil, this page starts with the agency responsible for your people's rights, including land demarcation and legal assistance.

The Fundação Nacional dos Povos Indígenas (Funai) is the Brazilian state's official Indigenous agency. Its mission is to protect and promote the rights of Indigenous peoples in Brazil, and its headquarters are in Brasília.

Sources: Funai: a Funai.

Background: read the Brasil chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.

Need help right now?

  • CVV, apoio emocional (24 horas, gratuito): call 188.

Crisis and safety

CVV: Centro de Valorização da Vida

Open to anyone

Apoio emocional e prevenção do suicídio, gratuito e sigiloso, 24 horas, por telefone, chat ou e-mail. (Free, confidential emotional support and suicide prevention, 24 hours.)

Who it is for
Qualquer pessoa no Brasil. (Anyone in Brazil.)
How to start
Ligue 188 de qualquer telefone, ou use o chat no site.

Call 188 cvv.org.br (chat)

Checked November 20, 2026. Source

Peoples, Nations and their governments

Funai: Fundação Nacional dos Povos Indígenas

For Indigenous people Brasília

O órgão indigenista oficial do Estado brasileiro, que protege e promove os direitos dos povos indígenas, inclusive a demarcação de terras e a assistência jurídica. (Brazil's official Indigenous agency: rights, land demarcation and legal assistance.)

Who it is for
Povos e comunidades indígenas no Brasil. (Indigenous peoples and communities in Brazil.)
How to start
Procure a Funai pelo site ou pela Ouvidoria na plataforma Fala.BR.

gov.br/funai

Checked November 20, 2026. Source
