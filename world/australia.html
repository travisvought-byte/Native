Australia: help and community for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people run their own health services across Australia: Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisations, started and governed by the local community. The first opened in Redfern, Sydney, in 1971. There is also a national crisis line answered by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people.

Sources: NACCHO (via the Royal Australian College of GPs) · Lifeline: 13YARN.

Need help right now?

  • 13YARN, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander crisis support: call 13 92 76.
  • In immediate danger, call 000.

Crisis and safety

13YARN

For Indigenous people

A free, confidential crisis line, 24 hours a day, answered by Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Crisis Supporters. Run by and for mob.

Who it is for
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people in Australia.
How to start
Call 13 92 76 from any phone, any time.

Call 13 92 76 13yarn.org.au

Health care

Aboriginal Community Controlled Health Organisations (ACCHOs)

For Indigenous people

Primary health care started and run by the local Aboriginal community through an elected board. NACCHO is the national body for about 150 of them.

Who it is for
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people and families.
How to start
Find the service nearest you on the map of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander health services.

Legal help and rights

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Legal Services

For Indigenous people

Community-controlled legal services in each state and territory: advice, court representation and support for people in contact with the justice system. NATSILS is their national body.

Who it is for
Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people.
How to start
Find your state or territory's service through NATSILS.

natsils.org.au

Your Nation and enrollment

Link-Up family tracing and reunion

Rules differ: ask

Help for members of the Stolen Generations to research family records, trace family, and be reunited with family, Country, community or gravesites, with support before, during and after.

Who it is for
Members of the Stolen Generations, their families and descendants. There is no Link-Up service in the ACT or Tasmania.
How to start
Contact the Link-Up service in your state or territory.
