Taiwan: help and community for Indigenous peoples of Taiwan

If you are from one of Taiwan's Indigenous peoples, or Pingpu and still seeking recognition, this page starts with the Council of Indigenous Peoples, the central government's council for Indigenous peoples.

Taiwan officially recognizes 16 Indigenous peoples, who numbered 629,456 people, 2.7% of the population, at the end of 2025. They are represented at every level of government, including the central government's Council of Indigenous Peoples. Lowland Pingpu peoples are still seeking recognition.

Sources: The Indigenous World 2026 (IWGIA): Taiwan.

Background: read the Taiwan chapter of The Indigenous World 2026, the yearly report of the International Work Group for Indigenous Affairs (IWGIA), on land, rights, health and recent events.
