Native Texas: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

The Alabama-Coushatta Tribe of Texas, the Ysleta del Sur Pueblo and the Kickapoo Traditional Tribe of Texas have their governments in Texas. If one of them is your Nation, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Dallas, which often serve any Native person; tribal health clinics; and tribal lands such as the Alabama-Coushatta Reservation and Ysleta del Sur Pueblo.

Native community and help in Texas

Health care

Urban Inter-Tribal Center of Texas

For Native people Dallas

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a medical clinic with a full range of outpatient care.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Call and ask how to become a patient or client, and which services are offered at the site nearest you.

Call 972-357-6615 Website

More: Indian Health Service directory of Urban Indian Organizations (phone numbers and websites)

Checked November 18, 2026. Source

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Texas

5 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
