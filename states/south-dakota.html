Native South Dakota: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

South Dakota is home to the governments of Nations such as the Cheyenne River Sioux Tribe, the Rosebud Sioux Tribe and the Sisseton-Wahpeton Oyate of the Lake Traverse Reservation. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Pierre and Sioux Falls, which often serve any Native person; Indian health hospitals in Eagle Butte, Pine Ridge and Rosebud; and tribal colleges such as Oglala Lakota College and Sinte Gleska University.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in South Dakota

Health care

South Dakota Urban Indian Health

For Native people Pierre and Sioux Falls

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a medical clinic with a full range of outpatient care.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Call and ask how to become a patient or client, and which services are offered at the site nearest you.

Call 605-339-0420 Website

31 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
