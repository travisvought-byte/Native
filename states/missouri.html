Native Missouri: urban Indian health, Indian health care and help

No federally recognized Nation has its government in Missouri, so if you are enrolled, your Nation's office is in another state; the Nations directory has every one. What is here: urban Indian programs in Kansas City, which often serve any Native person.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in Missouri

Recovery and wellness

Kansas City Indian Center

For Native people Kansas City

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering outpatient treatment for alcohol and drug use.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Call and ask how to become a patient or client, and which services are offered at the site nearest you.

Call 816-421-7608 Website

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Missouri

1 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
