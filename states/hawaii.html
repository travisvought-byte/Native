Native Hawaiians in Hawaiʻi: help, health care and Hawaiian home lands

If you are Native Hawaiian, start with the Office of Hawaiian Affairs, the Native Hawaiian Health Care Systems through Papa Ola Lōkahi, and the Department of Hawaiian Home Lands, which awards homestead leases. The Hawaiian home lands are listed further down.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in Hawaii

Health care

Papa Ola Lōkahi and the Native Hawaiian Health Care Systems

For Native people Honolulu

The Native Hawaiian health board. It works with five Native Hawaiian Health Care Systems, one for each island area, which serve Native Hawaiians in their communities.

Who it is for
Native Hawaiians and their families.
How to start
Contact the health care system for your island (linked below), or call Papa Ola Lōkahi.
Address
677 Ala Moana Blvd, Ste 720, Honolulu, HI 96813

Call 808-597-6550 papaolalokahi.org

More: Ke Ola Mamo (Oʻahu) · Hoʻōla Lāhui Hawaiʻi (Kauaʻi) · Hui No Ke Ola Pono (Maui) · Nā Puʻuwai (Molokaʻi and Lānaʻi) · Hui Mālama Ola Nā ʻŌiwi (Hawaiʻi Island)

Housing and homelessness

Department of Hawaiian Home Lands (DHHL)

Rules differ: ask Kapolei

The state department that manages Hawaiian home lands and awards homestead leases under the Hawaiian Homes Commission Act of 1921.

Who it is for
To apply for a homestead lease you must be at least 18 and at least 50% Native Hawaiian (descended from the people of the islands before 1778).
How to start
Call 808-730-0279 to begin a homestead application. The main line is 808-620-9500, Monday to Friday, 7:45 am to 4:30 pm.
Address
91-5420 Kapolei Parkway, Kapolei, HI 96707

Call 808-620-9500 dhhl.hawaii.gov: applications

Checked November 20, 2026. Source

Your Nation and enrollment

Office of Hawaiian Affairs (OHA)

For Native people Honolulu

A semi-autonomous state agency led by a board of trustees elected statewide. It is responsible for improving the wellbeing of all Native Hawaiians, regardless of blood quantum, and offers grants, loans and scholarship information.

Who it is for
Native Hawaiians, with no blood quantum requirement.
How to start
Call OHA, or use its website for loans (loans.oha.org) and scholarships.
Address
560 N. Nimitz Hwy #200, Honolulu, HI 96817

Call 808-594-1835 oha.org

Checked November 20, 2026. Source
