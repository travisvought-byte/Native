Native South Carolina: tribal Nations, community and state-recognized Nations

The Catawba Indian Nation has its government in South Carolina. If you are a citizen or a descendant, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: the South Carolina Native American Affairs (Commission for Community Advancement and Engagement) in Columbia; Indian Health Service clinics; and tribal lands such as the Catawba Reservation.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in South Carolina

Your Nation and enrollment

South Carolina Native American Affairs (Commission for Community Advancement and Engagement)

Open to anyone Columbia

The state agency that is South Carolina's official point of contact for Native American affairs. It handles state recognition of Native American tribes and groups and keeps the state's list of them, with contacts for each.

Who it is for
Native people, tribes and anyone with a question about Native American affairs in South Carolina.
How to start
Call or email the agency, or start from its list of state-recognized tribes and groups to reach a tribe directly.
Address
246 Stoneridge Dr., Ste. 250, Columbia, SC 29210

Call 803-333-9621 Email info@advance.sc.gov advance.sc.gov

Indian health clinics and hospitals in South Carolina

1 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
