New Hampshire: Nations and help for Native people

The Bureau of Indian Affairs directory lists no federally recognized Nation with its government in New Hampshire. Native people live in every state, and the national help on this page works here.

Local Native organizations, state programs and state-recognized Nations are not listed for New Hampshire yet. If you know one that belongs here, tell us.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Help that works in every state

  • Recovery and wellness: SAMHSA National Helpline · Wellbriety circles (White Bison) · Youth Regional Treatment Centers (IHS)
  • Children, youth and family: National Indian Child Welfare Association (NICWA) · The Indian Child Welfare Act (ICWA): your family's rights · UNITY: United National Indian Tribal Youth · We R Native: Ask Your Relatives
  • School and scholarships: American Indian College Fund scholarships · Cobell Scholarship · Tribal colleges and universities
  • Housing and homelessness: No place to stay tonight: emergency shelter · National Call Center for Homeless Veterans · Tribal housing programs · Section 184 Indian Home Loan Guarantee · BIA Housing Improvement Program
  • Food, money and bills: Food Distribution Program on Indian Reservations (FDPIR) · BIA financial assistance and social services · 211: local help with food, housing and bills · SNAP food benefits · Lifeline: a discount on phone or internet service
  • Work and training: Native job and training programs (WIOA Section 166) · Tribal vocational rehabilitation projects
  • Legal help and rights: Finding legal help (Native American Rights Fund guide) · Free civil legal aid near you
  • Your Nation and enrollment: Contact any federally recognized Nation (BIA Tribal Leaders Directory) · Tracing Native ancestry and enrolling in a tribe (BIA guide) · Indian boarding school records (National Indian Boarding School Digital Archive)
  • Elders: Elder programs run by tribes (Older Americans Act, Title VI) · Eldercare Locator
  • Veterans: Veterans Crisis Line · No VA copays for Native veterans · Native American Direct Loan (VA home loan on trust land)
