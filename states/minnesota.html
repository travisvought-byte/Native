Native Minnesota: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

Minnesota is home to the governments of Nations such as the Red Lake Band of Chippewa Indians, the Shakopee Mdewakanton Sioux Community of Minnesota and the Prairie Island Indian Community in the State of Minnesota. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Minneapolis and St. Paul, which often serve any Native person; Indian health hospitals in Cass Lake and Red Lake; and tribal colleges such as Leech Lake Tribal College and Red Lake Nation College.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in Minnesota

Health care

Indian Health Board of Minneapolis, Inc.

For Native people Minneapolis

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a medical clinic with a full range of outpatient care and outpatient behavioral health care.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Call and ask how to become a patient or client, and which services are offered at the site nearest you.

39 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area.
