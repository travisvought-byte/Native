Native California: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

California is home to the governments of Nations such as the Hoopa Valley Tribe, the Tule River Indian Tribe and the Yurok Tribe. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Santa Barbara, Bakersfield and Fresno, which often serve any Native person; tribal and Indian Health Service clinics; and tribal lands such as the Hoopa Valley Reservation and Tule River Reservation.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.
