Native Maine: tribal Nations, Indian health care and tribal lands

Maine is home to the governments of Nations such as the Penobscot Nation, the Passamaquoddy Tribe and the Houlton Band of Maliseet Indians. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: tribal health clinics and tribal lands such as the Penobscot Reservation and Passamaquoddy Trust Land.

Local Native organizations, state programs and state-recognized Nations are not listed for Maine yet. If you know one that belongs here, tell us.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Maine

6 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
