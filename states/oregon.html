Native Oregon: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

Oregon is home to the governments of Nations such as the Confederated Tribes of the Warm Springs Reservation of Oregon, the Cow Creek Band of Umpqua Tribe of Indians and the Coquille Indian Tribe. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Portland and Gresham, which often serve any Native person; tribal and Indian Health Service clinics; and tribal lands such as the Warm Springs Reservation and Umatilla Reservation.

Native community and help in Oregon

Health care

Native American Rehabilitation Association of the Northwest, Inc.

For Native people Portland

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a medical clinic with a full range of outpatient care, outpatient and residential treatment for alcohol and drug use, and child and family services.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Call and ask how to become a patient or client, and which services are offered at the site nearest you.

Call 503-224-1044 Website

More: Indian Health Service directory of Urban Indian Organizations (phone numbers and websites)

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Oregon

28 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
