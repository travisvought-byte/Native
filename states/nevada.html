Native Nevada: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

Nevada is home to the governments of Nations such as the Pyramid Lake Paiute Tribe, the Walker River Paiute Tribe and the Shoshone-Paiute Tribes of the Duck Valley Reservation. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Carson City and Reno, which often serve any Native person; tribal and Indian Health Service clinics; and tribal lands such as the Pyramid Lake Paiute Reservation and Walker River Reservation.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in Nevada

Health care

Nevada Urban Indians, Inc.

For Native people Carson City

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a clinic with a limited range of outpatient care.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Use the website to find the current phone number and address, then ask how to become a patient or client.

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Nevada

24 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
