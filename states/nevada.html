Nevada: Nations and help for Native people

Nations with their governments in Nevada, with the national help that works here too.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Help and community in Nevada

Health care

Nevada Urban Indians, Inc.

For Native people Carson City

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a clinic with a limited range of outpatient care.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Use the website to find the current phone number and address, then ask how to become a patient or client.

  • Work and training: Native job and training programs (WIOA Section 166) · Tribal vocational rehabilitation projects
  • Legal help and rights: Finding legal help (Native American Rights Fund guide) · Free civil legal aid near you
  • Your Nation and enrollment: Contact any federally recognized Nation (BIA Tribal Leaders Directory) · Tracing Native ancestry and enrolling in a tribe (BIA guide) · Indian boarding school records (National Indian Boarding School Digital Archive)
  • Elders: Elder programs run by tribes (Older Americans Act, Title VI) · Eldercare Locator
  • Veterans: Veterans Crisis Line · No VA copays for Native veterans · Native American Direct Loan (VA home loan on trust land)
