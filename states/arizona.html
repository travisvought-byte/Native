Native Arizona: tribal Nations, urban Indian health and Indian health care

Arizona is home to the governments of Nations such as the Navajo Nation, the Tohono O'odham Nation of Arizona and the San Carlos Apache Tribe. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: urban Indian programs in Flagstaff, Phoenix and Mesa, which often serve any Native person; Indian health hospitals in Chinle, Dilkon and Fort Defiance; and tribal colleges such as Diné College and Tohono O'Odham Community College.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.
