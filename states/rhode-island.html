Native Rhode Island: tribal Nations, Indian health care and tribal lands

The Narragansett Indian Tribe has its government in Rhode Island. If you are a citizen or a descendant, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: tribal health clinics and tribal lands such as the Narragansett Reservation.

Local Native organizations, state programs and state-recognized Nations are not listed for Rhode Island yet. If you know one that belongs here, tell us.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Rhode Island

2 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
