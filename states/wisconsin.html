Wisconsin: Nations and help for Native people

Nations with their governments in Wisconsin, with the national help that works here too.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in Wisconsin

Health care

Gerald L. Ignace Indian Health Center

For Native people Milwaukee

An Urban Indian Organization funded by the Indian Health Service. The Indian Health Service lists it as offering a medical clinic with a full range of outpatient care.

Who it is for
American Indian and Alaska Native people living in or near the city. Ask what proof of tribal affiliation or descent it needs.
How to start
Call and ask how to become a patient or client, and which services are offered at the site nearest you.

Call 414-383-9526 Website

Indian health clinics and hospitals in Wisconsin

27 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
