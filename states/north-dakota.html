Native North Dakota: tribal Nations, local help and Indian health care

North Dakota is home to the governments of Nations such as the Three Affiliated Tribes of the Fort Berthold Reservation, the Standing Rock Sioux Tribe of North & South Dakota and the Spirit Lake Tribe. If your Nation is here, its office is on this page with a way to reach it. Also on this page: the North Dakota Indian Affairs Commission in Bismarck; Indian health hospitals in Belcourt and Fort Yates; and tribal colleges such as Cankdeska Cikana Community College and Nueta Hidatsa Sahnish College.

Need help right now?

  • Thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Veterans: dial 988, then press 1.
  • Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member, or sexually assaulted: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483.
  • Being made to work or sell sex: call 1-888-373-7888 or text 233733.
  • In immediate danger, call 911.

Native community and help in North Dakota

Your Nation and enrollment

North Dakota Indian Affairs Commission

Open to anyone Bismarck

The state office that links North Dakota's governor and state agencies with the tribes in the state. Its website gathers links on tribal enrollment and other resources.

Who it is for
Tribes, Native people and anyone working with tribes in North Dakota.
How to start
Use the contact page on its website.

Indian health clinics and hospitals in North Dakota

13 places listed by the Indian Health Service. Clinics run by the Indian Health Service or a tribe mostly serve members and descendants of federally recognized tribes; some serve everyone in the area. Urban Indian programs often serve any Native person. Call first and ask whether you can be seen and what to bring.
