Native community and help in Alaska

Crisis and safety

Careline Alaska (crisis line)

Open to anyone Fairbanks

Alaska's own crisis line, run by Alaskans for Alaskans. It offers free, confidential help 24 hours a day, every day, for anyone thinking about suicide, in crisis, isolated or depressed, and supports people who have lost someone to suicide. Calls and texts to 988 from a 907 area code are answered by Careline.

Who it is for
Anyone in Alaska.
How to start
Call 1-877-266-4357 any time, or call or text 988. You can also text 4help to 839863, Tuesday to Saturday, 3 pm to 11 pm. In immediate danger, call 911.

Call 1-877-266-4357 Text 4help to 839863 carelinealaska.com

More: Alaska Division of Behavioral Health: 988 and Careline work together

CITC Victim Services (formerly Alaska Native Justice Center)

For Native people Anchorage

Free, confidential help for victims and survivors of domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking and other crimes. These services moved from the Alaska Native Justice Center to Cook Inlet Tribal Council in October 2025.

Who it is for
Alaska Native and American Indian people in Southcentral Alaska. Call to ask about help in your situation.
How to start
Call 907-793-3550, email VictimServicesInfo@citci.org, or walk in. In immediate danger, call 911.
Address
2550 Denali St, Anchorage, AK 99503

Health care

Alaska Native Medical Center (ANMC)

Rules differ: ask Anchorage

The statewide hospital for Alaska Native and American Indian people, giving acute, specialty, primary and behavioral health care. It is jointly owned and managed by the Alaska Native Tribal Health Consortium (ANTHC) and Southcentral Foundation, and works with rural health facilities across Alaska. ANTHC runs patient housing for people who travel in for care.

Who it is for
Alaska Native and American Indian people living in Alaska. Call Eligibility at 907-729-2353 to check what you need to show.
How to start
The main switchboard answers 24 hours. The Emergency Department (907-729-1729) is open 24 hours. For care coordination, call toll-free 855-482-4382.
Address
4315 Diplomacy Dr, Anchorage, AK 99508

Call 907-563-2662 anthc.org

More: Southcentral Foundation: Emergency Department, 24 hours

Checked November 20, 2026. Source

Southcentral Foundation (Nuka System of Care)

Rules differ: ask Anchorage

An Alaska Native-owned health care organization serving about 70,000 Alaska Native and American Indian people in Anchorage, the Matanuska-Susitna Borough and nearby villages. Its Nuka System of Care, managed by Alaska Native people, covers physical, mental, emotional and spiritual wellness, including primary care, behavioral health and traditional healing.

Who it is for
Alaska Native and American Indian people in the Anchorage Service Unit. Ask about eligibility when you call.
How to start
For primary care, call the Anchorage Native Primary Care Center at 907-729-3300 (4320 Diplomacy Drive). The administrative office's toll-free number is 800-478-3343.
Address
4501 Diplomacy Drive, Anchorage, AK 99508

Call 907-729-4955 southcentralfoundation.com
