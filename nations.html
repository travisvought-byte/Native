Nations directory: find your Nation's office

Looking for your Nation's office, to ask about enrollment, services or events? Search by name, town or state; each Nation's government office is here with a phone number and website where the directory has one.

This list is built from the Bureau of Indian Affairs Tribal Leaders Directory, retrieved November 17, 2026. The Bureau counts 575 federally recognized tribes as of January 30, 2026, and lists some bands and communities separately. It says the directory is not the official list (that is the notice it publishes in the Federal Register) and may contain errors. A Nation is listed under the state where its government office is; its homelands and citizens may reach far beyond that state.

Names appear as the Bureau lists them. Nations recognized by a state and not on the federal list are in their own section below, added one state at a time from the official list of each state. Nations with neither kind of recognition are not listed yet; write to us to be added. If you speak for a Nation and want its listing changed, write to travis@vethomeguard.org.
