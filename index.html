      Need help right now?

      • United States: thinking about suicide, or in any crisis: call 988 or text 988, any time. Hurt or threatened by a partner or family member: call or text the StrongHearts Native Helpline, 1-844-762-8483. In immediate danger, call 911.
      Crisis lines in other countries
      • Canada: Hope for Wellness, for all Indigenous people, 1-855-242-3310. Emergency: 911.
      • México: Línea de la Vida (crisis, 24 horas), 800 911 2000. Emergency: 911.
      • Australia: 13YARN, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander crisis support, 13 92 76. Emergency: 000.
      • Aotearoa New Zealand: Need to talk? Free call or text, any time, 1737 (call or text). Emergency: 111.
      • Brasil: CVV, apoio emocional (24 horas, gratuito), 188.
      • Perú: Línea 113 Salud, opción 5: salud mental (24 horas, gratuita), 113, opción 5.
      • Chile: Línea de prevención del suicidio *4141 (24 horas, gratuita, desde celulares), *4141.
      • Taiwan: 1925 Care Hotline, suicide prevention (24 hours, free), 1925.
      • Philippines: NCMH Crisis Hotline (24 hours), 1553.
      • South Africa: SADAG Suicide Crisis Helpline (24 hours, free), 0800 567 567.
      • Colombia: Línea 106, salud mental (24 horas, gratuita), 106.
      • Ecuador: Línea 171, opción 6: primeros auxilios psicológicos (gratuita), 171, opción 6.
      • India: Tele-MANAS mental health helpline (24 hours, free, 20 languages), 14416.
      • Malaysia: Talian HEAL 15555, Ministry of Health mental health crisis line (daily, 8 am to midnight), 15555.
      • Argentina: Línea de salud mental 0800-999-0091 (24 horas, gratuita), 0800-999-0091.
      • Paraguay: Línea 155 Salud Mental (24 horas, gratuita), 155. Emergency: 911.
      • Panamá: Línea 147, Mides (24 horas, gratuita), 147.
      • Thailand: Mental Health Hotline 1323, Department of Mental Health (24 hours, free), 1323.
      • Costa Rica: Línea 1322, Despacho de Apoyo Psicológico (24 horas), 1322. Emergency: 911.

      Who made this, and who it needs

      This guide was built by Travis Vought from what Nations, governments and Indigenous organizations publish about themselves. It is not a tribal government and does not speak for any Nation. It covers the United States in the most detail, with Ohio and Michigan the deepest, and now reaches 32 more countries and homelands: Canada, México, Australia, Aotearoa New Zealand, Sápmi (Norway, Sweden, Finland), Brasil, Perú, Guatemala, Chile, Kalaallit Nunaat (Greenland), Taiwan, Japan, Philippines, Botswana, Namibia, South Africa, Colombia, Ecuador, Bolivia, India, Kenya, Nepal, Bangladesh, Malaysia, Indonesia, Argentina, Paraguay, Panamá, Thailand, Cambodia, Costa Rica and Tanzania.

      It needs Indigenous people to look it over. If you work for a Nation, a tribal program or an Indigenous organization, or you know something here is wrong or missing, write to us. Read how the site is put together.
