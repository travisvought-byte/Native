Get a free page for your community

If you work for a Nation or a tribal program, or you organize a Native community group, powwow, language class or business, Nina's Map will set up a page for you, free. You say what goes on it; it is there for your news, your events and what you need.

Ask for a page by email See an example page

What you get

  • Your own page at an address like https://ninas-map.com/communities/your-name.html, with your name, logo and photos.
  • Your message, in your own words, and your announcements and events. Past events drop off on their own.
  • Your programs, your contact details and your links.
  • A "How you can help" section for what you need right now: donations, volunteers, people at your events, customers, people to share the word, or a list of supplies.
  • Links to your page from your listing on Nina's Map, from the Community page, and, when you post an ask, from Support Native-led groups.

Free, with no catch

  • There is no fee, now or later, and nothing to sign up for.
  • The page is yours. It says what you want it to say, and it says on the page that you wrote it and keep it up.
  • You can change it whenever you like, and have it taken down at any time, for any reason. Just ask.
  • Nina's Map does not speak for your Nation or your group, and does not add anything to your page that you did not send or approve.
  • The page has no ads. Visits are counted without cookies, as on the rest of the site, and nothing about your visitors is sold.

What to send

Send whatever you have; a name and a few sentences are enough to start.

  • Your group's name as you want it written, and your town and state or country.
  • A few sentences about who you are and who you serve.
  • The phone number, email and website you want shown. Only contacts you want public.
  • Upcoming events and news, with dates.
  • Programs you run, and how people can join.
  • How people can help, and until when.
  • Your logo and a few photos, if you like, with a line about each. Send only photos you have the right to share, and only of people who agreed to be shown.
  • Your name and your role, and a way to confirm the request with you.

How we help you set it up

  1. You send what you have by email, in any form: a flyer, a website, a few lines.
  2. We check that the request comes from your group, using your group's public phone or email. For a Nation, that means its government office. A page is never made for a group that did not ask for one.
  3. We put the page together from what you sent, and send you a preview.
  4. You approve it, or tell us what to change. It goes live only when you say so.
  5. Send updates any time: a new event, a new ask, a new photo. Each page shows the date it was last updated.

If you are not sure what to say, or what kind of help to ask for, say so, and we will work it out with you.
